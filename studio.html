PHILOSOPHICAL PILLARS

The Architecture of Uncompromising Equilibrium

Most architectural practices treat structural engineering as an afterthought to form. At STRATAFORM, engineering is the form. We believe a building’s aesthetic power stems directly from how honestly it transfers its loads into the planet.

TENET 01

Raw Structural Monumentality

We celebrate exposed massing, post-tensioned diagrids, and high-strength concrete cores. Structural authenticity creates an undeniable sense of timeless permanence.

TENET 02

Acoustic & Tactile Refinement

Interior spaces are mechanically and acoustically decoupled from exterior winds. Hand-quarried Italian travertine, brushed bronze, and acoustic laminates provide tranquil sanctity.

TENET 03

Carbon-Negative Lifecycle

We mandate low-carbon pozzolanic binder concrete mixes, geothermal thermal mass loops, and demountable structural steel nodes that achieve true circular engineering.

MATERIAL PROVENANCE

The Physical Material Library

Four essential geological and metallurgical elements curated for durability, seismic absorption, and timeless luxury.

GEOLOGICAL STONE

Roman Vein-Cut Travertine

Quarried directly from historic beds in Tivoli, Italy. Cut parallel to bedding planes to reveal linear porous graining with high thermal inertia.

DENSITY: 2,450 kg/m³ • SLAB: 30mm Calibrated
METALLURGY

Brushed Architectural Bronze

Marine-grade copper-tin-zinc alloy extruded into knife-edge solar fins and mullions. Forms an organic protective patina without pitting.

ALLOY: CuSn8 / C52100 • FINISH: 240-Grit Directional
COMPOSITE

C90 Ultra-Performance Concrete

Advanced pozzolanic binder blend incorporating 40% ground granulated slag, lowering embodied carbon by 52% while hitting 90 MPa compressive strength.

STRENGTH: 90 MPa (28 Days) • CARBON: -52% Embodied
ENVELOPE

Acoustic Triple Curtain Glazing

Dual argon gas cavities with structural acoustic PVB interlayers, blocking 48dB of exterior aerodynamic roar while delivering 0.8 W/m²K thermal efficiency.

U-VALUE: 0.8 W/m²K • ACOUSTIC: Rw 48 dB
