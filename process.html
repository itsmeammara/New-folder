PHASE 01 // SUBTERRANEAN

Geotechnical & Deep Foundations

Every STRATAFORM landmark is anchored directly into unyielding bedrock. We employ continuous flight auger micropiles, secant retaining walls, and a monolithic post-tensioned foundation mat to eradicate differential settlement across high-seismic zones.

PHASE 02 // SUPERSTRUCTURE

Monolithic Superstructure & Core

The structural spine of each tower rises via hydraulic self-climbing slipform systems. Ultra-high performance C90 concrete with post-tensioned tendon arrays provides unmatched lateral rigidity, while outrigger trusses bridge the core directly to perimeter columns.

Parametric Façade & 450T TMD

Envelope installation occurs synchronously with superstructure ascent. Wind-tunnel optimized bronze louvers shed vortex shedding, while a 450-tonne pendular tuned mass damper suspended on the 54th floor dampens harmonic building resonance.
